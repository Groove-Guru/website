  1. step 01 / 06

    count the bar

    house and techno count in fours. sixteen steps is one bar; light a kick on every beat and you’ve built four-on-the-floor — the grid everything else sits on.

    light steps 1, 5, 9 and 13 — one kick on every beat.

    a cue is a bookmark you drop from. set it on the one — the first beat of a phrase — and the drop lands exactly where the room expects it.

03 / the coach

a calm voice, at the right moment.

the coach waits for something worth saying — a key clash, a missed phrase turn, a deck sliding off the grid — says it once, then gets out of the way. think yoga teacher, not hype man.

listens first

it only speaks to what it can read off the wire: key, phrase, pitch, sync, on-air. no guessing at vibes.

one note per phrase

a correction, then quiet. no running commentary while you’re mid-blend.

plain words

4A into 11B clashes. 5A sits better.

short enough to hear over the monitors.

04 / keys & phrases

keys tell you what fits. phrases tell you when.

pick a key on the wheel. neighbours light up — one step round, or straight across between minor and major. anything further is a clash the coach will call.

a clean key change still lands wrong if it arrives mid-phrase. step 3 drills the when; step 4 uses this wheel.

05 / on the wire

it listens. it doesn’t touch.

pro dj link gear talks udp on the booth lan. groove guru sits on the same switch as a passive listener and builds its picture of the mix from what the decks already broadcast. open protocol work, documented in the repo.
